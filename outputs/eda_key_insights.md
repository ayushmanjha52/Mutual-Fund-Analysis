
## Day 3 - Key EDA Insights (10 Points)

1. **NAV Growth**: Top funds showed strong growth in 2023, followed by corrections in 2024-2025.
2. **AUM Concentration**: SBI and a few large fund houses dominate total industry AUM.
3. **SIP Trend**: Monthly SIP inflows are growing steadily with seasonal spikes.
4. **Age Demographics**: Majority of investors fall in the 26-45 age group.
5. **Gender Split**: Male investors significantly outnumber female investors.
6. **Geographic Concentration**: Maharashtra, Delhi-NCR, and Telangana contribute highest investments.
7. **City Tier**: T30 cities have higher investor participation than B30 cities.
8. **Category Preference**: Equity funds receive the highest net inflows.
9. **Correlation**: Strong positive correlation between 3-year returns and Sharpe ratio.
10. **Folio Growth**: Industry folio count has grown consistently from 2022 to 2025.
